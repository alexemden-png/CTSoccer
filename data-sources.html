Data Sources

CT Soccer Hub pulls together information from a few different places — some of it live from public sports data, some of it hand-researched, and some of it submitted directly by the clubs themselves. This page explains exactly where everything comes from, how current it is, and what to do if something looks wrong.

1Live match data — TheSportsDB

Four Connecticut clubs — Hartford Athletic, CT United FC, AC Connecticut, and CT Rush (USL2) — are tracked through TheSportsDB, a public sports data service. This works in two layers:

  • Each club's season record and roster is refreshed by hand periodically and stamped with a "Data Synced" date on its page, so you can always see exactly how current it is — it'll flag itself if that date is more than a week old.
  • The most recent final score and next scheduled match are pulled live, in real time, every time you load that club's page — not on a fixed schedule, but fresh at the moment you're looking at it.

No other Connecticut clubs currently have a public data feed available, which is why this list is short — it's a limitation of what data exists publicly, not a choice about which clubs matter.

2Club directory — CJSA

The directory of Connecticut youth clubs (over 160 of them) — names, cities, and websites — is sourced from the Connecticut Junior Soccer Association's official club listing at cjsa.org. This is directory information only, not stats or results, and it's updated in occasional batches rather than continuously, so a club's listed website or contact link may lag behind if they've recently changed it.

3Club profiles — manually researched

The more detailed profiles for youth and academy clubs (coaching staff, tryout info, contact details) were researched by hand from each club's own website and social media. These aren't refreshed on any schedule — they reflect what was publicly available at the time they were written, and get updated when a club reaches out with a correction (see below) or when we do another research pass.

4Match results — submitted by Team Managers

Some match results you'll see on a club's page were entered directly by that club's own Team Manager, not pulled from any outside source. This isn't open to anyone — a person has to request Team Manager access for a specific club through their account, and that request is personally reviewed and approved before they can submit anything.

How current these are depends entirely on how often that club's manager updates them.

5Corrections — from the clubs themselves

Several clubs have already reached out directly to correct details we had wrong — for example, Inter CT FC contacted us to fix their location, colors, and coaching structure after our initial research got parts of it wrong. We treat that as the most reliable source there is: if a club tells us something about themselves is inaccurate, we fix it.

Spot something wrong?

If you spot outdated or incorrect information anywhere on the site — a wrong coach's name, an old score, a club that's missing entirely — use the form below. A real person reads every submission.
