About CT Soccer

CT Soccer (ctsoccerhub.com) is a hub for Connecticut soccer — one place to find real club profiles, live results, tournaments, coaching clinic listings, and a community feed for the people who make up the state's soccer scene, from Hartford Athletic's professional roster down to town-level youth clubs.

Who it's for

CT Soccer is built for four overlapping groups:

⚽
Players
📋
Coaches
💼
Team Managers
🏇
Fans

Whether you're a player following Hartford Athletic's season, a parent trying to find the right youth club or an upcoming tryout, a coach comparing CJSA course pricing, or a team manager keeping your roster and results up to date, CT Soccer is meant to be the fastest way to find what you're looking for.

What's on the site

  • Club Dashboard — profiles for Connecticut's professional, semi-professional, and youth clubs, with live results for Hartford Athletic, AC Connecticut, and CT Rush pulled automatically from TheSportsDB.
  • Tournaments — a browsable list of CJSA and EDP tournaments across the state.
  • Coaching Clinics — every CJSA and US Soccer coaching course available in Connecticut, with real pricing.
  • Soccer Assistant — a guided recommendation tool to help match players and families with the right club or course.
  • News — season storylines and auto-updating match results for CT's real clubs.
  • People & Community — a directory and social feed for the CT Soccer community, once you create a free account.

How our data is sourced

We're not making this up as we go — club and league data comes from a mix of real, named sources:

  • CJSA (Connecticut Junior Soccer Association) — the club directory and coaching course listings are sourced from cjsa.org.
  • US Soccer — national coaching license pricing and course structure via the US Soccer Learning Center (learning.ussoccer.com).
  • TheSportsDB — a public sports-data API that powers live match results and fixtures for Hartford Athletic, AC Connecticut, and CT Rush.
  • Official league standings — USL Championship, MLS Next Pro, NPSL, and USL League Two tables sourced from each league's own site (uslchampionship.com, mlsnextpro.com, npsl.com, uslleaguetwo.com).
  • Club websites & submissions — youth and competitive club contact info, tryout details, and program descriptions are gathered from clubs' own public websites. If you run a CT club and see something out of date, let us know and we'll fix it.

Some club descriptions and season stats are still being verified against live sources — see our Terms of Service for the full accuracy disclaimer before relying on anything here for a real decision (like a tryout date). When in doubt, confirm directly with the club.
