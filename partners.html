Where CT Soccer's
data comes from

CT Soccer isn't affiliated with a sponsor program — this page instead credits the real governing bodies, data providers, and clubs whose information populates the platform.

Governing Bodies
CJSA
Youth Governing Body

Connecticut Junior Soccer Association

The state's official youth soccer association. Powers our Full Directory of 163 member clubs and the real coaching license courses in the Coaching Clinics section.
USSF
National Governing Body

U.S. Soccer Federation

Sets the Grassroots-through-Pro coaching license pathway and runs the Learning Center where every course on this site is actually booked.
Live Data Provider
DB
Live Results

TheSportsDB

Free, open sports data API. Every "Live" badge you see on Hartford Athletic, AC Connecticut, and CT Rush pulls its results and fixtures from here, refreshed every 5 minutes.
LC
Coaching Registration

US Soccer Learning Center

The official registration system for every coaching course listed here. CT Soccer links out — it never processes a coaching registration itself.
