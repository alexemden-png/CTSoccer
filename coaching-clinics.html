Coaching Education
& Clinics

Real US Soccer and Connecticut Junior Soccer Association (CJSA) coaching license courses — from free introductory modules through the B License. Filter by skill tier or sort by price.

Prices and formats reflect real 2026 CJSA/US Soccer offerings. Exact dates rotate — always confirm on the US Soccer Learning Center before registering.

How the Value Score works: like a ticket deal-score, it's not just the price — it's price divided by instructional hours, ranked against every other course on this page. Cheapest-per-hour courses score as a Great Deal; priciest-per-hour score as Premium. It says nothing about a course's quality — a B License is worth far more than a Grassroots module regardless of score.
