About Inter Connecticut FC

Inter Connecticut FC operates across three branches serving Fairfield, New Haven, and Litchfield Counties, with pathways from Micro Soccer (U6) through U19 and beyond. The club offers academy, travel, and premier programs through its Union, Norwalk, and Litchfield Hills branches.

Inter CT FC operates across three branches serving Fairfield, New Haven, and Litchfield Counties. The Union Branch (New Haven, based in Milford) is directed by Stephen Bell. The Norwalk branch is led by four directors: Tom Robinson (Boys Pre-11v11), Anthony Quinn (Boys 11v11), Marc Davis (Girls Pre-11v11), and Marty Walker (Girls 11v11).
