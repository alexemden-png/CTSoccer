About Ole FC

Ole FC, founded in Monroe in 2004 by Rodrigo Nunes, offers developmental programs from age 18 months through U14. The club runs the Ole Developmental Academy (ODA) and partners with Connecticut FC for higher-level travel opportunities.

Known for its welcoming community culture and wide range of age-appropriate programs, Ole FC operates the Ole Training Center in Monroe and serves families across southwestern Connecticut with Stars programs, goalkeeping clinics, and adult leagues.
