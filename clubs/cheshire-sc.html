About Cheshire Soccer Club

One of the oldest and most established youth soccer clubs in Connecticut, serving Cheshire since 1975.

Cheshire Soccer Club has developed generations of Connecticut soccer players across recreational and travel programs. The club offers age-appropriate competition for every skill level.
