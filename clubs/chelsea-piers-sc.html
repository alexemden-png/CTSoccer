About Chelsea Piers FC

Chelsea Piers FC is the competitive youth soccer program within the acclaimed Chelsea Piers Connecticut sports complex in Stamford.

Operating out of one of the premier sports facilities in the Northeast, Chelsea Piers FC provides elite youth training and ECRL competition for Fairfield County players.
