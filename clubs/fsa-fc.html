About FSA FC

FSA FC (Farmington Sports Arena Football Club) competes in the Elite Clubs National League (ECNL) — one of the highest-level youth soccer leagues in the country. Based at a 130,000 sq ft indoor facility with 9 outdoor fields, FSA FC serves U9 through U19.

Founded in 2009, FSA FC rapidly rose to become one of Connecticut's premier youth clubs. The club hosts the FSA Columbus Cup each fall and the Northeast Spring College Showcase, which draws college coaches from across the country.
