What's New

Recent updates and new features on CT Soccer, newest first. This page is updated by hand as things ship — if something looks missing, it probably just hasn't been added here yet.

August 2026
🔔
New
Notifications for clubs you follow
Following a club now syncs to your account, and a notification bell on your Dashboard and Account page lets you know when a club you follow has a new match result posted.
🛡️
Improved
Team Manager access now requires approval
Requesting to manage a club's match results and roster now goes through a review step instead of being granted instantly, so match results stay trustworthy.
👥
New
Member directory (People)
Search and browse other players, coaches, and fans on CT Soccer, filter by role or club, and follow the people you want to keep up with — only public profile info is ever shown.
⚽
New
Team Manager match result submissions
Approved Team Managers can now submit real match results for their club, visible to anyone browsing that club's page.
☁️
Improved
Accounts moved to the cloud
Accounts, profiles, and club follows are now stored server-side instead of only in your browser — sign in from any device and everything is right where you left it.
